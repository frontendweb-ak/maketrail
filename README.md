# Maketrail

**Find solutions. Build further.**

Maketrail is a proposed global marketplace for discovering, evaluating, purchasing, and integrating creator-built software solutions.

The marketplace brings together:
- **Buyers** — development agencies, startups, and software businesses looking for reusable software.
- **Creators** — independent developers and software studios publishing products they own and can support.
- **Implementation partners** — qualified developers who help buyers integrate, customize, and maintain solutions.

> Product and business direction are being validated. This repository begins with discovery, documentation, and operating practices; it does not imply that the marketplace scope or technical architecture is final.

## Working principles

1. Validate buyer demand and creator supply before building the marketplace.
2. Prefer real transactions and observed behavior over opinions and assumptions.
3. Label unverified claims, assumptions, and decisions explicitly.
4. Never publish products without appropriate ownership and distribution rights.
5. Treat security, licensing, compatibility, payments, and trust as core product concerns.
6. Keep the initial architecture as simple as possible until evidence justifies complexity.
