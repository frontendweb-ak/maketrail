# Target user personas

**Status:** Hypotheses to validate  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-24

These are research personas, not claims about confirmed customers. Update them with anonymized interview evidence.

## Persona 1 — Agency owner / delivery lead

**Role:** Owner, CTO, or delivery director at a small or mid-sized software agency.

**Job to be done:** Deliver client projects predictably while controlling engineering cost and reducing repeated implementation.

**Pain hypotheses:**
- Teams rebuild common modules across projects.
- Evaluating third-party code takes time.
- Unclear licenses and abandoned repositories create risk.
- A cheap download may become expensive to integrate or maintain.

**Desired outcomes:**
- Find a solution that fits a real project.
- Assess compatibility, rights, maintenance, and support quickly.
- Know total product plus integration cost.
- Deliver successfully without hidden obligations.

**Trust signals:**
- Clear license and ownership.
- Working demo and current documentation.
- Supported versions and known limitations.
- Transparent verification evidence.
- Creator responsiveness and real buyer feedback.

**Research questions:**
- How often does this happen?
- What was the last module rebuilt or purchased?
- What was the actual time and cost?
- Who approves purchases?
- What evidence changes the decision?

## Persona 2 — SaaS founder / engineering lead

**Role:** Founder, CTO, or engineering lead at an early-stage or growing software company.

**Job to be done:** Ship product capabilities quickly without creating unsustainable maintenance or security risk.

**Pain hypotheses:**
- Limited engineering capacity.
- Need for dependable infrastructure and business modules.
- Uncertainty about whether third-party solutions will fit the existing stack.
- Concern about vendor lock-in, updates, and long-term ownership.

**Desired outcomes:**
- Shortlist compatible solutions.
- Understand integration effort and lifecycle costs.
- Acquire appropriate rights.
- Get help when internal capacity is limited.

**Research questions:**
- Which capabilities are blocking roadmap delivery?
- What is built in-house versus purchased?
- What is the cost of delay?
- What technical and legal review is required?

## Persona 3 — Independent software creator

**Role:** Independent developer with one or more reusable products.

**Job to be done:** Turn reusable work into revenue without losing control of intellectual property or spending all their time on marketing and sales.

**Pain hypotheses:**
- Hard to reach buyers with purchase intent.
- Unclear pricing and licensing expectations.
- Support and maintenance burden after sales.
- Existing platforms may not support implementation services or product-specific trust evidence.

**Desired outcomes:**
- Publish work they own.
- Set clear license and support terms.
- Reach relevant buyers.
- Earn from products and optional implementation.
- Manage versions and buyer expectations.

**Research questions:**
- What product have they already built?
- Can they prove ownership and distribution rights?
- Has anyone paid for it?
- What support can they realistically provide?
- What marketplace terms would make participation worthwhile?

## Persona 4 — Small software studio

**Role:** Small product studio or specialist development company.

**Job to be done:** Distribute reusable products and generate qualified implementation opportunities.

**Pain hypotheses:**
- Product discovery and service lead generation are disconnected.
- Buyers need confidence in technical fit and support.
- Maintaining multiple distribution channels is costly.

**Desired outcomes:**
- A credible product catalog presence.
- Qualified buyer conversations.
- Product and service revenue with clear responsibilities.
- Repeat customers and predictable support boundaries.

## Persona 5 — Implementation partner

**Role:** Creator or qualified developer integrating a third-party product.

**Job to be done:** Deliver a defined integration with clear scope, access, and acceptance criteria.

**Pain hypotheses:**
- Product quality and documentation are inconsistent.
- Scope expands because compatibility assumptions were unclear.
- Responsibility between product creator and implementer is ambiguous.

**Desired outcomes:**
- Defined versions, environment, and deliverables.
- Clear access and license permissions.
- Agreed price, timeline, and acceptance criteria.
- Explicit support and maintenance responsibilities.

## Persona validation record

For every interview, record:
- Persona and company type.
- Relevant recent behavior and concrete example.
- Frequency and impact of problem.
- Existing alternative and why it was chosen.
- Actual budget/spend or engineering effort, if shared.
- Buying authority and timeline.
- Evidence that would establish trust.
- Permissioned follow-up and next step.

Do not record unnecessary personal or confidential information.
